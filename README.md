# CASESTUDY-MANUFACTURING-LIMITED-MANAGEMENT-ACCOUNTS-FINANCIAL-ANALYSIS

## Overview
A complete monthly management accounts pack prepared from scratch using a raw trial balance for a fictional manufacturing company, Case Study Manufacturing Limited, which produces juice, soft drinks and alcoholic beverages. The project shows how raw financial data can be turned into reporting that supports business decisions.

## Objective
To go beyond recording transactions and show how financial statements reveal true business performance, not just revenue.

## Scope of Work
- Trial Balance to Financial Statements: mapped and grouped trial balance accounts into the income statement, financial position, cash flow and supporting notes
- Income Statement: prepared and analysed actual results against budget, with variance percentages and commentary
- Statement of Financial Position: compared current balances with the prior year-end
- Statement of Cash Flows: reconciled operating, investing and financing activities to the closing cash balance
- Executive Dashboard: summarised revenue by product category, revenue trend, EBITDA and net income
- Supporting Notes: 21 schedules, including loan roll-forward, tax liability, inventory, fixed assets and retained earnings

## Key Findings
- Revenue came in about 45% below budget, but the deeper pressure came from heavy finance costs and cash tied up in inventory and supplier advances.
- Customer advances rose sharply, a positive sign of buyer confidence.
- Heavy investment in plant and equipment doubled fixed assets, reducing cash and increasing borrowings. This showed the trade-off of building capacity ahead of revenue.
- Operating cash flow stayed positive despite a net loss, showing the gap between profit and cash.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Trial balance analysis and account mapping
- Preparing integrated financial statements from raw ledger data
- Budget vs. actual variance analysis
- Working capital and cash flow analysis
- Debt and finance cost tracking
- Management reporting and dashboard design
- Translating numbers into business insight

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel: Filters, VLOOKUP, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Data Validation

## Key Takeaway
Revenue shows activity, but profit, cash flow and working capital show whether a business is truly healthy.
